## Data controller

The data controller is Jarosław Kucharski. Contact for data matters: [jaroslaw.kucharski@iamfront.dev](mailto:jaroslaw.kucharski@iamfront.dev).

## What data and why

Your name, email address, topic and message – only to reply to your message and continue the conversation.

The legal basis is my legitimate interest in replying to your message (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR) and, when you write about a collaboration or a job, steps taken before entering into a contract (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR). Providing the data is voluntary, but without it I can't reply.

## Who receives the data

- **Cloudflare, Inc.** (USA) – hosts the site and forwards emails sent to my iamfront.dev address.
- **Web3Creative** (India), the operator of [Web3Forms](https://web3forms.com/dpa) – receives messages from the contact form, filters spam and forwards them to me by email. For spam protection, your IP address and email address may be passed to CleanTalk and Akismet.
- **Google LLC** (USA) – provides my Gmail inbox, where all messages end up.

Data is transferred to the USA under the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework, in which Cloudflare, Inc. and Google LLC participate. Transfers to Web3Creative are based on the standard contractual clauses approved by the European Commission, included in the Web3Forms data processing agreement. I don’t sell or share the data with anyone else.

## How long I keep it

For as long as we’re in touch, and then no longer than 12 months. If we start working together – for as long as we work together, and then until any claims become time-barred.

Web3Forms keeps a copy of form messages on its servers for 30 days and then permanently deletes it. If you ask me to delete your data, I’ll also ask Web3Forms to delete it.

## Your rights

You have the right to access, correct and delete your data, to restrict or object to its processing and to data portability. Just write to the address above. I don’t make automated decisions or profile anyone based on your data. You can also lodge a complaint with the Polish data protection authority (President of the Personal Data Protection Office, UODO).

## Cookies and settings

This site uses no cookies. Your chosen theme and animation setting are saved only in your browser (localStorage) and never sent anywhere.

Like any host, Cloudflare’s servers keep technical logs (e.g. IP addresses) needed to run and secure the site. The legal basis is my legitimate interest in keeping the site secure (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).
