## Data controller

The data controller is Jarosław Kucharski. Contact for data matters: [jaroslaw.kucharski@iamfront.dev](mailto:jaroslaw.kucharski@iamfront.dev).

## What data and why

Your name, email address, topic and message – only to reply to your message and continue the conversation.

The legal basis is my legitimate interest in replying to your message (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR) and, when you write about a collaboration or a job, steps taken before entering into a contract (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR). Providing the data is voluntary, but without it I can't reply.

## Who receives the data

The site is hosted by Cloudflare, Inc. (USA). Form messages are sent through the Web3Forms service, and emails sent to my iamfront.dev address are forwarded by Cloudflare. Both end up in my Gmail inbox, provided by Google LLC (USA).

Data is transferred to the USA under the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework, in which Cloudflare, Inc. and Google LLC participate, and under the terms of the Web3Forms service. I don’t sell or share the data with anyone else.

## How long I keep it

For as long as we’re in touch, and then no longer than 12 months – unless we start working together.

## Your rights

You have the right to access, correct and delete your data, to restrict or object to its processing and to data portability. Just write to the address above. You can also lodge a complaint with the Polish data protection authority (President of the Personal Data Protection Office, UODO).

## Cookies and settings

This site uses no cookies. Your chosen theme and animation setting are saved only in your browser (localStorage) and never sent anywhere.

Like any host, Cloudflare’s servers keep technical logs (e.g. IP addresses) needed to run and secure the site.
